Part I

The Brand Story

Why Pro Lash exists, who it's for, and the line it draws — in the brand's own voice.

01

Your world

You love your extensions. The fill every three weeks, the length, the way your eyes look done before you've done anything else. But somewhere in the last year or two, you noticed it — puffy, red lash lines. Natural lashes falling out quickly and growing back slowly. A lash line that, underneath the extensions, feels thinner than it used to.

You didn't imagine it. And you're not the only one who's felt like her real lashes got ruined chasing the look she wanted.

02

What's broken

The beauty industry gave you two options. Take a "lash break" — weeks of feeling under-done, waiting for your natural lashes to recover, while your calendar doesn't care that you have somewhere to be. Or reach for a growth serum — and hope it's not one of the ones people describe as "aggressive," the ones that left them with eyes that "stung so badly they had to wash it off," or worse.

Both options ask you to gamble something — your look, or your comfort. Neither one was built for someone who's not giving up her extensions.

03

What we believe

You shouldn't have to choose between the extensions you love and the real lashes underneath them. And "clean" shouldn't mean slow, and "effective" shouldn't mean risky. Most of the category's biggest names lean on prostaglandin-style actives to get their results — a mechanism with real, documented irritation complaints attached to it. We think that trade-off was never necessary. It was just the only one anyone offered you.

04

How it actually works

Pro Lash is prostaglandin-free — no exceptions, no fine print. Vegan. Built on peptides, hyaluronic acid, vegetable keratin, biotin, and pumpkin seed oil, at a formulation confirmed safe to wear underneath your extensions. It's not chasing speed at any cost. It's chasing real strength in your actual lashes, applied nightly, while you keep living your actual routine — extensions and all.

05

What changes

Stronger, longer, healthier natural lashes underneath whatever you're wearing on top of them. The freedom to skip a fill without feeling under-done. Eventually — if you want it — the option to go bare-lashed and still feel like yourself. Not "no more extensions." Just: extensions and real lashes that can hold their own.

06

Who we are

Pro Lash was formulated by someone who spent six years as a working lash technician — watching client after client come in with lash lines that had quietly, gradually thinned out underneath their extensions. Not because anyone did anything wrong. Because nobody had built a serum for the person who wasn't going to stop wearing extensions to fix it.

So we built one. Organic, vegan, prostaglandin-free, and clinically proven — priced at €19.99 for one, €35 for two, because the goal was never to out-premium the big names. It was to finally give this exact problem an actual answer.

07
We're not anti-extensions.
We're anti pretending your real lashes don't matter underneath them.

Positioning

Where Pro Lash stands

Pro Lash Serum is for women who love their lash extensions but are watching what those extensions do to the natural lashes underneath — women who want thicker, stronger real lashes without giving up the extensions or risking the eye irritation that comes with the prostaglandin-based serums most competitors use. Formulated by a six-year lash technician, Pro Lash is organic, vegan, prostaglandin-free, and clinically proven, with peptides, hyaluronic acid, vegetable keratin, and biotin doing the work — built to be worn safely alongside extensions. Priced accessibly (€19.99–35) against category leaders charging £35–38 for a comparable single unit.

The Audience

Damaged-Lash Dana

Primary avatar. Women 22–38, already spending on lash maintenance, beauty-engaged on Instagram/TikTok, skeptical by default because she's been burned by overpromising products before.

Who she is

A current or recent lash-extension wearer who's noticed real damage to her natural lashes underneath.

Who she wants to become

Someone who could skip a fill without feeling under-done — extensions by choice, not by necessity.

What she fears becoming

Visibly thin, patchy, "used up" lashes — a state she associates with letting herself go.

Her #1 objection

"Will this irritate my eyes the way [a competitor] did for other people?"

Secondary, overlapping segment: young mothers, newly ingredient-conscious across their whole routine, actively seeking "clean" formulations — a market independently valued at $3.2B in pregnancy-safe skincare alone.

The Argument Line

The six beliefs

Every asset — ad, email, page, script — advances one belief. Never all six at once.

Belief 1

I believe that… my thin, damaged lashes are a direct result of extension wear — not just "how my lashes are." Bridge: ~30% of extension wearers experience documented lash damage.

Belief 2

I believe that… I don't have to choose between wearing extensions and having healthy natural lashes underneath them. Bridge: a formula confirmed safe to wear under extensions.

Belief 3

I believe that… a lash serum can actually work without the eye irritation I've heard about. Bridge: confirmed prostaglandin-free — the highest-leverage belief in the set.

Belief 4

I believe that… organic and vegan doesn't mean weaker or slower results. Bridge: clinically proven results + founder credibility.

Belief 5

I believe that… this brand understands my exact routine better than a generic beauty company would. Bridge: the six-year lash-technician founder story.

Belief 6

I believe that… €19.99–35 is a fair price, not a red flag. Bridge: "no markup for a risk you don't want."

Proof & Honesty

What we claim. What we never claim.

No claim, stat, or proof point appears anywhere in Pro Lash copy unless it's sourced in project-knowledge.md or deepresearch.md. If we want a claim and can't source it, we flag the gap — we don't invent it.

✓ What we claim

  • Prostaglandin-free — confirmed
  • Vegan — confirmed, incl. vegetable (plant-derived) keratin
  • Ingredients: peptides, hyaluronic acid, vegetable keratin, biotin, pumpkin seed oil
  • Clinically proven results — confirmed by founder (general claim, no specific figure yet)
  • Formulated by a six-year working lash technician
  • Safe to wear underneath lash extensions — confirmed
  • ~30% of extension wearers experience documented lash damage (sourced, category-wide statistic — not a Pro Lash-specific claim)
  • €19.99 single / €35 two-pack pricing

✕ What we never claim

  • Any specific %, sample size, or study detail behind "clinically proven" — not documented yet
  • A guarantee — terms not yet defined
  • Any connection to postpartum / hormonal lash thinning — not researched or confirmed
  • Disparaging claims about named competitors beyond what's directly sourced
  • Specific bottle size / ml or applicator type — not yet confirmed
  • Subscription or payment-plan availability — not yet defined

Highest-leverage gap to close: get the sample size, % results, and timeframe behind "clinically proven" — it upgrades the strongest claim in the guide from an assertion into a quotable stat.

Do

Use Fraunces italic for exactly one emphasis word per heading, and for big stat numerals. Keep body copy in Manrope, never letter-spaced.

Don't

Don't set full sentences in the accent italic — it's a signature touch, not a paragraph face. Don't use the mono face above 13px.
