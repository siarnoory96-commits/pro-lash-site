# Offer Brief — Pro Lash Serum

## Product
- **What it is (literal description):** A vegan, organic, prostaglandin-free lash-growth serum, formulated to strengthen and grow natural eyelashes — including for people currently wearing lash extensions.
- **Format / delivery:** Physical topical serum, applied to the lash line (applicator type and bottle size in ml still **[UNVERIFIED]** — confirm with founder).
- **Core ingredients / components / modules (confirmed by supplier spec sheet, Beijing Charming Biotechnology Co., Ltd.):** Aqua (57%), Sodium Hyaluronate (10%), Pentylene Glycol (3%), Panax Ginseng Extract (3%), Swertia Japonica Extract (3%), Panthenol (3%), Cucurbita Pepo (Pumpkin) Seed Extract (2%), Serenoa Serrulata (Saw Palmetto) Fruit Extract (2%), Biotinoyl Tripeptide-1 (4%), Myristoyl Pentapeptide-17 (4%), Oligopeptide-10 (2%), Tocopherol (2%), Biotin (3%), Acetyl Tetrapeptide-3 (2%). Prostaglandin-free — confirmed by the real ingredient list (no prostaglandin analogue present). **Correction:** earlier docs listed "vegetable keratin" as an ingredient — the actual supplier spec sheet has no keratin of any kind. That claim is retired; nothing in the real formula depended on it.

## The Big Promise
- **The one-sentence promise:** Stronger, longer, healthier natural lashes — even while you wear extensions — without the eye-irritation risk of the prostaglandin-based serums the big names use.
- **The result, quantified where possible:** Founder states results are **clinically proven**. Category-standard results (per competitor research) show visible change in 3–4 weeks, fuller results by 6–12 weeks — usable as a directional timeline. **[Still worth getting from the founder: the specifics behind "clinically proven" — sample size, % improvement, timeframe, who conducted the study. That level of detail is what turns a claim into a stat a copywriter can actually quote (e.g., "87% saw longer lashes in 4 weeks*") rather than a generic assertion.]**
- **The timeframe:** Nightly/daily application; first visible change expected within weeks, not days — set this expectation up front to protect trust (see Necessary Beliefs).

## Unique Mechanism
- **Name of the mechanism:** Working name — "Extension-Safe Formula" or "Lash-Tech Formula" (to be finalized in Lesson 2/3; should encode both the oil-free-enough/extension-compatibility angle and the organic/vegan/prostaglandin-free angle).
- **What it is in plain English:** A serum built by someone who spent six years applying lash extensions and watching what they do to natural lashes underneath — so it's formulated to be safe to use *while wearing extensions* and to actively rebuild what extension wear damages, using four named peptides, hyaluronic acid, biotin, ginseng, and saw palmetto instead of a prostaglandin-style active.
- **Why it works (the logical argument):** Extensions damage roughly 3 in 10 wearers' natural lashes (breakage, thinning, or loss — see `deepresearch.md` Section 4). Most fixes ask you to stop wearing extensions to heal. Pro Lash is designed to let you keep wearing them *and* heal — solving the actual behavior pattern instead of asking the customer to abandon it.
- **Why it works better than existing category solutions:** Category leaders (Nanolash, UKLash) use prostaglandin-style actives with documented irritation and adverse-event complaints. Pro Lash is confirmed prostaglandin-free, vegan, and clinically proven — a fully backed version of the claim most "clean" competitors only gesture at.
- **Why competitors can't / haven't replicated it:** None of the three named competitors are sourced as founded by a working lash technician, and none combine organic/vegan/prostaglandin-free positioning with explicit extension-compatibility messaging in available research — this pairing is currently uncontested ground, though Trybello (plant-based, hormone-free) is the closest analog and should be watched.

## Positioning Statement
Pro Lash Serum is for women who love their lash extensions but are watching what those extensions do to the natural lashes underneath — women who want thicker, stronger real lashes without giving up the extensions or risking the eye irritation that comes with the prostaglandin-based serums most competitors use. Formulated by a six-year lash technician who watched this exact damage happen to client after client, Pro Lash is organic, vegan, prostaglandin-free, and clinically proven, with four named peptides, hyaluronic acid, biotin, ginseng, and saw palmetto doing the work instead of a prostaglandin analogue — and it's built to be worn safely alongside extensions. Priced accessibly (€29.49–47.99) against category leaders charging £35–38 for a comparable single unit, because the goal isn't to out-premium the big names, it's to give the person facing this problem daily a solution that actually respects what her lash routine already looks like.
